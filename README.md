# TrackBound-Game
TrackBound is a cooperative first-person survival and management game for up to four players built around a customizable, endlessly generated railway adventure. The core experience contrasts serene daytime exploration with intense nighttime survival, requiring players to physically interact with their locomotive, maintain machinery, and gather vital resources. A comprehensive loop involves piloting the train through persistent biomes, stopping to scavenge abandoned facilities, processing materials onboard, and defending against dangerous nocturnal threats. While the game offers recommended specializations like engineers and medics, it avoids strict role locks to ensure flexible cooperation. Ultimately, the train serves as the central hub, functioning simultaneously as home, defense system, and mobile workshop as players progress through increasingly hazardous territory.
